# Kinetic Shift
### A precision platformer made in godot for hack club's thirdspace week 8. 
Your only weapon is your dash. every enemy can be insta-killed with your dash, and you can momentarily change directions during the hitstop. also, every kill grants you another dash mid air.

AD - left right movement

Spacebar - jump/ cling to wall when in air

WS (when clinging to a wall) - move up/down

Shift - dash
